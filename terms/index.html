A self-control tool

Dead Zone helps you limit your own phone use. You choose the limits, so make sure the apps you block are not ones you need for work, safety, health or emergencies.

  • Dead Zones. A Dead Zone is a recurring blocker with set times and days, an ongoing commitment. It blocks the apps you choose at the times you set. Once a Dead Zone starts, it runs to the end unless you end it with a groupmate's unlock code, or, with nobody else in your group, by typing a sentence. Deleting your account also removes every block on your iPhone.
  • Lock-ins. A Lock-in is a one-off, unscheduled stretch off your phone that you commit to with your group. A Lock-in blocks the apps you choose when you start it, for at least 15 minutes. Your group is told when you start one, others in your group can join, and your group is told if you end it early with a groupmate's unlock code.
  • Daily Limit. Your Daily Limit counts time on your whole phone. When you reach it, the apps you chose stay blocked until midnight.
  • Your groups. You can be in up to 10 groups. Each sees the same things about you. Nothing from Screen Time is shared unless you choose to share. Sharing covers your daily total, streak, blocked app attempts, protection changes and the nightly notification, and you can change it in Settings. Your status (in a Dead Zone, in a Lock-in or free), your Lock-ins, slaps, hugs and the names and times of your Dead Zones are visible to your groups either way. Dead Zone never tracks your location.
  • Friends. You can add friends by username and invite them to your groups. A friend who is not in a group with you cannot see your activity or give you unlock codes.
  • Changing your settings. Loosening a setting, such as unblocking apps, deleting a Dead Zone or raising your Daily Limit, needs a groupmate's unlock code, or, with nobody else in your group, a sentence you type. The same applies to ending a running Dead Zone or Lock-in early. A code works straight away and expires after 72 hours. A request can include a short reason (up to 140 characters) that the groupmate sees. A code can come from anyone you share a group with.

Dead Zone relies on Apple's Screen Time framework. iOS always lets you turn off Screen Time access or delete the app, and Dead Zone cannot prevent this. Screen time totals are estimates based on what iOS reports, and blocking may not work if iOS, your settings or your device prevent it.
