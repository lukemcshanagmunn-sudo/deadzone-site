How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Dead Zone Plus subscription. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

On the paywall, tap Restore purchases.

How do I report or block someone?

In the app, tap the three-dot control next to the person, then choose Report or Block. We aim to review reports within 24 hours.
