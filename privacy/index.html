Privacy policy

This policy explains what Dead Zone collects, why, who can see it, where it is stored and how to delete it.

Dead Zone is made by Luke McShanag-Munn, an independent developer in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia ("we", "us"). Contact: luke.mcshanagmunn@gmail.com.

Our approach

Some small businesses are exempt from the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Whether or not that applies to us, we handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. We collect only what Dead Zone needs to work, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Dead Zone uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block apps.

  • The apps and categories you choose are stored by iOS as tokens that Dead Zone cannot read. We never see which apps you choose or use.
  • Per-app usage times are not available to Dead Zone and are not collected.
  • Your block list, your adult website filter setting and the day you last reached your daily limit are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your onboarding answers are saved on your iPhone while you set up the app.

What we collect

We collect information from you when you use the app, from your iPhone through Apple's frameworks, and from Apple when you subscribe.

Account

  • Your email address, used to sign in with an email code or a password.
  • Sign-in records kept by our authentication provider.

Profile

  • Your display name, if you set one.
  • Your birth year, used for the life-in-months view and to confirm you are 18 or over.
  • Your goals, the things you chose in onboarding.
  • Your daily limit and your iPhone's time zone.
  • Your “Share with your group” choice, made in onboarding and changeable in Settings.
  • The reason you wrote for using Dead Zone, if you add one.

Screen time totals

  • A daily total of your screen time. iOS tells Dead Zone when your use passes set points during the day, and Dead Zone saves the highest point reached as that day's total. It does not say which apps were used.
  • Your streak of days under your daily limit.

Dead Zones and lock-ins

  • Each Dead Zone's name, start and end times and days.
  • Lock-ins you start or join: length, start, end and whether you finished.

Activity your group can see

  • Counts of attempts to open a blocked app, and whether a Dead Zone or the daily limit blocked them. Not which app.
  • When Screen Time access is turned off or back on, when a Dead Zone is deleted, and when blocking is switched off.
  • Slaps and hugs you send and receive.

Requests to change your settings

When you ask to turn off the adult website filter, unblock apps, delete or change a Dead Zone, or raise your daily limit: what you asked for, the groupmate you asked (if any), the code they share with you, the sentence you typed (which includes your own phone time, such as "I spent 3h 10m on my phone yesterday"), and when it was approved, used or expired.

Groups

  • Groups you create or join, the invite code, and when you joined.
  • When someone leaves a group, a short notice with their display name, kept for the people still in it.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, a push token for your iPhone. It is removed when you sign out.

Subscriptions

  • Your Dead Zone account ID is linked to our subscription provider so the app knows whether you have Dead Zone Plus. If you subscribe, Apple handles payment and our subscription provider receives your purchase records. We never receive your card details.

Dead Zone does not include analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To block apps, apply your daily limit and show your numbers.
  • To run groups, lock-ins, slaps, hugs and notifications.
  • To manage subscriptions.
  • To review reports and keep Dead Zone safe and secure.
  • To fix problems and answer your questions.

We use your information only for these purposes, or as the law requires or allows. We do not send marketing emails.

Who can see your data

Your group. People in a group with you can see your display name, attempts to open blocked apps, lock-ins, slaps and hugs, and changes to your protection such as turning off Screen Time access or deleting a Dead Zone.

Share with your group. While this is on, your group also sees your daily screen time total, your streak, your daily limit and your place on the Ledger. Turn it off in onboarding or in Settings at any time and your group sees none of those. This is enforced by our servers, not only hidden in the app, and it also keeps you out of the nightly notification. It changes only what your group sees about you.

Unlock requests. A groupmate you ask to approve a change sees what you asked for, such as unblocking apps or raising your daily limit, whether or not you share your screen time. Groupmates never see which apps you use, your email address, your birth year, your goals or your push token.

Leaving a group. You can leave your group at any time on the Accountability tab without deleting your account. Your membership is removed straight away, the group stops seeing your activity and you stop seeing theirs, and the people still in the group are told you left. Your Dead Zones and daily limit stay on. A change you are waiting to unlock keeps the same wait and moves to typing a sentence instead of a code. If you created the group, it passes to the longest-standing member. If you were the last member, the group is deleted.

Notifications to your group. Dead Zone sends your group notifications about some of this activity. For example, a nightly notification names the groupmate with the most screen time that day and their total. Only people who share their screen time with the group can be named.

Service providers. These providers help run Dead Zone. They handle data for us to provide their service:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions. Our database is hosted in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Expo: delivers notifications and app updates. It receives your push token and basic device details such as the app version.
  • Apple: delivers notifications, takes payments and provides the Screen Time framework.
  • RevenueCat: tracks subscription status. It receives your Dead Zone account ID, purchase records and basic device details.
  • Our email provider, currently Google (Gmail): sends sign-in codes to your email address. We plan to move to Resend and will update this page when we do.

Others. We share information only if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal request, or to protect someone's safety.

Data stored outside Australia

Our database is in Japan. Our providers are based in the United States and may also process data in other countries where they or their sub-processors operate. We choose established providers that are bound by their terms to keep data secure and use it only to provide their service to us. Your information is not stored in Australia.

Security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections.
  • Rules in our database let other users see only what is described above. Server keys are never in the app.
  • At Dead Zone, only Luke McShanag-Munn has administrative access.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you as soon as we can and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account, and delete it when you delete your account.

  • Our database. Deleting your account removes your data from our live database straight away. Our current Supabase plan does not keep daily backups or point-in-time recovery copies of the database, so no backup copies of your data are kept that we could restore. Supabase may keep short-lived operational logs and its own infrastructure recovery copies, which it deletes in the normal course. If we move to a plan with backups, we will update this page with how long backups are kept.
  • Sign-in emails. Because sign-in codes are currently sent from our Gmail account, a copy of each email (your address and a one-time code) is kept in that account's sent mail. We delete these copies at least once a month.
  • Purchase records. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations, such as tax law.
  • Emails you send us. We keep your messages for as long as we need to deal with your request, then delete them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings at any time. Deletion is immediate. It removes your account, profile, screen time totals, streak, Dead Zones, lock-ins, group memberships, blocked-attempt counts, protection changes, requests, slaps and hugs. If you created a group that still has other members, the group passes to the longest-standing member. If you were the only member, the group is deleted. Deleting your account also removes every block on your iPhone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Dead Zone Plus subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Your choices

  • Turn off “Share with your group” in Settings to stop your group seeing your screen time total, streak, daily limit and place on the Ledger.
  • Leave your group on the Accountability tab. Your account and your blocks stay.
  • Turn off notifications in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Turn off Screen Time access in your iPhone's Settings. Dead Zone can't block apps without it, and your group is told.
  • Delete your account in Settings.

Access and correction

You can also change most of your details in the app. We may need to confirm it's you, for example by replying to the email address on your account. There is no charge. We will respond within 30 days. If we can't do what you ask, we will tell you why, where the law allows, and how to complain.

If you live outside Australia

Dead Zone is made in Australia and offered in the countries where it is available on the App Store. If the law where you live gives you other rights, such as to delete, restrict or move your data or to object to how it is used, email us and we will help you use them.

Age

Dead Zone is for adults 18 and over, and the app does not let you set a birth year under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.
